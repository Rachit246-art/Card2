FAQEVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

How does the creation process work?
After choosing your design and completing your order, you'll receive access to our intuitive details questionnaire. There, you can upload your wedding photos, ceremony details, venue address, dress code, timeline and chosen music. Our team creates your private draft in 3 to 5 business days.
Can we make changes after seeing our draft?
Yes, absolutely! Every plan includes unlimited revisions to your text, photos, maps, and details until you are 100% delighted with the result.
How do our guests access the invitation?
You receive a private, elegant link that you can share via WhatsApp, SMS, email, or your wedding website. It opens instantly on any smartphone, tablet, or desktop computer without requiring guests to install an app or create an account.
Can our invitation be bilingual or in another language?
Yes! We support every language in the world, including dual-language and multilingual invitations with a clean toggle button for your international guests.
How long will our digital invitation stay active online?
Your invitation remains active for up to 6 months after your wedding date so you and your guests can look back at the photos, timeline, and memories.
Is there an expiration date on the Gift Card?
Our gift cards have no expiration date and can be redeemed whenever the couple is ready to plan their wedding or announce their save the date.
